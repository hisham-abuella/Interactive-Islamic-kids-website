A Special Baby

Long ago, a special baby was born. His name was Ibrahim. Even as a child, Ibrahim was very smart and always wondered about the world around him.

👆 Tap to learn a fun fact!
💡

Ibrahim is known as "Khalilullah" - the Friend of Allah! He is one of the greatest prophets.

Looking at the Sky

Ibrahim wanted to teach his people who the true Lord is. One night, he looked at a bright star and asked them, "Is this my lord?" But when the star disappeared, he said, "I do not love things that disappear!"

What did Ibrahim see next?

The Beautiful Moon

Then Ibrahim saw the moon, big and bright! "This is my lord!" he said to his people. But when morning came, the moon also disappeared. Ibrahim was showing his people that something which disappears cannot be our Lord.

📱 Tap the moon to see what happens!

The Mighty Sun

When the sun rose, Ibrahim said to his people, "This is my lord - it's the biggest!" But when evening came, even the powerful sun went away.

Ibrahim declared to his people that he turned in worship to Allah alone, the Creator of the heavens and the earth!

💡 What did Ibrahim teach his people?

Ibrahim showed his people that only Allah - who created the stars, moon, and sun - deserves to be worshipped!

Ibrahim's people worshipped statues made of stone. Ibrahim knew this was wrong. "How can you worship things that can't hear, see, or help you?" he asked them.

The Great Fire

The angry people built a huge fire and threw Ibrahim into it! But Ibrahim stayed calm. He trusted Allah completely.

How do you think Ibrahim felt?

+2 ⭐

Allah's Amazing Miracle!

But Allah protected Ibrahim. In the Quran, Allah tells us:

قُلْنَا يَا نَارُ كُونِي بَرْدًا وَسَلَامًا عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ

"We said: O fire, be coolness and safety for Ibrahim." (Surah Al-Anbiya 21:69)

The fire became cool and safe! Ibrahim walked out unharmed. SubhanAllah!
