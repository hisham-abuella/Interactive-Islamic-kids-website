# Game spec: Al-Fil, find it in the picture (mockup 08)

Status: MOCKUP at `UI/mockups/games/08-al-fil-seek.html`. The scene is inline SVG and its art is due for the graphics redesign. Needs sign-off from the scholar and the learning designer.

- **Learning goal:** After playing, the child can retell Surat Al-Fil in order (the army with elephants, the plan that lost its way, the flocks of birds, the stones of baked clay, the eaten straw) and say that Allah protected His House.
- **Age band:** 5-9 ("Little finder"); 8-11 ("Sharp eyes" asks for 3 elephants and 3 bundles of straw).
- **Tied to:** `surah-al-fil.html`, whose ayat, translations, explanations, parent question and recap are all copied from it. The page's own plates (`images/surahs/surah-al-fil/verse-N.webp`) are reused as the reward for each ayah.
- **Core loop:** hear an ayah's meaning, find and tap what it describes, and watch the scene change as that ayah comes true.
- **Round (5 ayat plus a closing find):**
  1. Find the elephant(s).
  2. Find the army's map; its arrow tangles ("their plan lost its way").
  3. Tap three far-off flocks; they fly in "flock after flock".
  4. Tap the stones under each flock; they fall.
  5. The camp fades; find the straw.
  6. Find the Kaaba; it glows ("Allah protects His House").
  After each find the page's explanation and plate appear. The ending shows the whole surah to recite together, plus the recap.
- **Pause point:** before the closing find, the page's parent question: "The biggest army was stopped by the smallest birds. What does that tell you about Allah?"
- **Failure:** a wrong tap names what was tapped ("That is a palm tree.") and gives the step's hint. After 3 misses the targets shimmer. There is no counter and no penalty.
- **Replay:** flock positions vary between three layouts; the tier changes the counts.
- **EN/AR/RTL:** the panel mirrors. The scene is a picture and is not mirrored, since it is not directional.
- **Accessibility:** every scene object, including the distractors, is a focusable `role=button` with a name, so keyboard play is still a search. Hit circles are 48px or more at phone width. Reduced motion is respected.

## Guardrail checklist
- [x] **No people drawn at all**: the army is shown only by tents, banners, a map and elephants. No prophet, Companion or angel appears.
- [x] Scripture and explanations copied verbatim; no ayah is altered.
- [x] The Kaaba is shown as a simple building only.
- [x] Scholar (approved 2026-11-03, with conditions): stones fall onto tents and sand only, never elephants, no impact; the Kaaba only as a plain hand-drawn SVG with no writing.
- [x] Scholar (approved 2026-11-03): the "map with an arrow" is our visual metaphor for كيدهم (their plan), not a narrated detail. Please approve or replace it.
