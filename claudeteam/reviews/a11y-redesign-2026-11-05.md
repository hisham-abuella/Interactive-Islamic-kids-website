# Accessibility audit: Illuminated Page v2 app redesign (2026-11-05)

Accessibility Auditor, read-only. Branch `ui-refero-refresh` at c7cb2d3. Spec `UI/DESIGN.md`.
Earlier conditions: `claudeteam/reviews/a11y-ui-ideas-2026-11-03.md`.

**Method.** I used headless Chrome 156 over CDP against a no-cache `http.server` on `UI/`, at 390x844
(touch emulation) and 1280x900, in EN and AR, by day and in bedtime mode. I ran four scripts:

- **Contrast sweep.** It walks every visible text node and composites the alpha backgrounds up through
  the ancestors. On the reader pages it walks every slide, and it also runs with both sheets open.
  It covered 9 pages x 2 widths x 2 languages x 2 themes.
- **Keyboard walk.** Real Tab key events. At each stop it records the outline style, the ring contrast
  against the surface behind, and whether the element is covered (`elementFromPoint`).
- **Behaviour probes.** It drives the sheets, the reader, the quiz, the verse narration and the name modal.
- **Reflow and text size.** It checks 320 px, 640 px (1280 at 200% zoom), and a root font size of 200%.

All ratios are WCAG 2.x relative luminance. For gradients I quote the worse stop.

### 2. Focus visibility and order

**FAIL.** The base ring is `--focus`, 3 px, not animated, at styles.css:971-981. It passes almost everywhere:
5.06 on parchment, 5.37 on surface-1, 4.53 on surface-2, and 8.02 to 9.62 in bedtime. That covers the
tab bar (inset -4px, 5.06), the sheets, the segmented controls, the transport and the side rail. Hover lift is
mirrored on `:focus-visible` (app.css:429-432, stories.css v2). Tab order is logical in EN and AR:
skip link, app bar, tabs, then content. In the reader the order is: close, Aa, read-aloud, settings,
story links, play, next.

Failures:
- **Bedtime toggle and language toggle in every app bar: 1.75.** `header .lang-toggle-btn:focus-visible,
  header .bedtime-btn:focus-visible { outline-color: var(--ornament-light) }` (styles.css:1052-1055) was
  written for the dark v1 header, but `.app-bar` is also a `<header>`. `.bedtime-btn:focus-visible`
  (styles.css:1255-1258) does the same. Measured on Home, Stories, Quran and every surah page, by day.
  The same rule also scales the bedtime button on focus (styles.css:1249-1253).
- **Focus hidden under the tab bar (2.4.11):** "See all", the stories/quran "Play Story" and "Start Learning"
  buttons, and verse "Listen" buttons were all focused at y 774-842, behind the 64 px bar at 390 px. Nothing
  sets `scroll-padding-bottom`; app.css:1021-1025 sets only `scroll-margin-top`. Fix:
  `html { scroll-padding-block-end: calc(var(--tabbar-h) + var(--safe-bottom) + 16px) }` below 1024 px,
  and the transport height on `body.reader`.
- quran.html progress badge ring is `--gold`: **2.99** on the card (stories.css:2029-2032).
- Yusuf v1 header links and nav-toggle ring is `--focus` on the turquoise header: **1.16**.
- **Focus lost to `<body>`:**
  - On the last page, Enter on "Next page" disables the button (stories.js:938). Measured in EN and AR.
  - Answering a mini-quiz option wrongly disables it while it has focus (stories.js:198, 205). Measured.

### 3. Sheets as dialogs

**PASS.** Both are native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`: `#homeSheet` (index.html:227) and
`#readerSheet` (stories.js:701-735). Measured `:modal` true, which supplies aria-modal semantics without
the attribute. Each is labelled by its h2 (Settings / Reading settings, localised). Tab stays inside the
dialog; the only extra stop is the browser chrome. Esc closes. Focus returns to the trigger: #homeSettings,
#nameEdit and #readerSettings were all verified. Arrow keys do not turn pages while the sheet is open.

The legacy name modal is not one of these sheets. It FAILS; see item 12.

- `aria-current="page"` is on the right tab on every page (checked in all 25 shell pages).
- Labels are visible text, 12-13 px, text-2 at 5.71.
- Items are 130x64 at 390 and 96x64 on the rail.
- The bar pads with `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`; `viewport-fit=cover` is on all 30 pages.

Failures and nits:

- Nit: on surah detail pages the Quran tab says `aria-current="page"`. `"true"` would be more accurate.

### 5. Story reader semantics

**PASS on the core, FAIL on the leftovers.**
- **Inert hidden pages:** with 16 slides, 15 were `inert` and `aria-hidden` (stories.js:913-919). The active
  page has `aria-roledescription="page"` and `aria-label="Page 1 of 16"`, localised to «صفحة ١ من ١٦».
- **Live region:** polite and atomic (stories.js:678-683). It is silent on load. After Next it says
  "Page 2 of 16: The Important Message"; in AR «صفحة ٢ من ١٦: الرسالة المهمة», under lang=ar. Focus stays on Next.
- **Labelled controls:** close, Aa ("Text size: medium", which updates), read-aloud (`aria-pressed`, state
  verified to toggle), settings (`aria-haspopup=dialog`), previous, next and play. Names are swapped to
  Arabic in AR.
- **Direction:** keys and swipe are mirrored in RTL (ArrowLeft goes forward in AR, verified). The glyphs
  carry `.flip-rtl`.

Remaining failures:
- **Adam and Ibrahim effects toggle `#soundToggle`.** Its accessible name is the emoji "🔊"; the `title` is
  not used because content wins. It has no `aria-pressed` and no Arabic label (story-prophet-adam.html:54,
  ibrahim:54). This was on my 11-03 must-fix list.
- **Stars counter** reads as a bare "0" inside the toolbar, with no label (story-prophet-adam.html:47-50).
- **Quiz, choice, emotion and activity feedback is not announced.** No live region exists
  (stories.js:179-214; `.choice-feedback`, `.emotion-feedback`, `.activity-feedback`). Measured: after a
  wrong answer the "Try again!" text changes outside any live region. This was must-fix 2 on 11-03.
- **`.tap-to-reveal` and `.drag-item` are still `<div>`s with `tabIndex -1`.** A keyboard cannot operate them.
  This was must-fix 3.
- **Play button flips its label and `aria-pressed` together** (audio-narration.js:537-538). It announces as
  "Pause, toggle button, pressed". Keep one constant label ("Listen to this page") with `aria-pressed`,
  or change the label without `aria-pressed`. The mini-player play does the same (surah-narration.js:216-217).

### 6. Verse highlight

**PASS.** I stubbed `Audio` to hold the playing state and measured:
- `.is-narrating` and `aria-current="true"` are set.
- The 3 px `::before` bar sits on `inset-inline-start` in `--focus`: 4.69 on reward-wash by day, 7.58 in bedtime.
- The wash changes, the button changes to "■ Stop" with `aria-pressed=true`, and the mini player reads
  "Verse 2 of 7".
- Focus stays on the tapped button. Off-screen scrolling uses `behavior: reduced ? 'auto' : 'smooth'`
  (surah-narration.js:196-197).

So the highlight is not colour-only. In AR the feature is disabled by `AR_VERSE_AUDIO`, so I could not
exercise the RTL bar position at runtime. It relies on the logical property.

Should fix:
- Every verse button is named "▶ Listen", with the glyph spoken (surah-narration.js:152). Seven identical
  names; use "Listen to verse 3".
- The mini-player text is a polite live region (surah-narration.js:267) that changes on every verse.
  That is acceptable, but drop the region while idle.

### 7. Reduced-motion paths

**PASS, with one FAIL.**
- With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, `document.getAnimations()` returns 0 on every page, after a page
  turn, and with the sheet open. Yusuf leaves one zero-length animation.
- The global block covers `::view-transition-*` (styles.css:1005-1010), and no page uses View Transitions yet.
- `goToSlide` calls `scrollBy` with `behavior: 'auto'` (verified by intercepting it; stories.js:877-878).
  surah.js:255-257 and surah-narration.js:196-197 are correct as well.
- Without the preference, **no infinite animation exists on any page**, so WCAG 2.2.2 now passes.
  On 11-03 Adam had 30 running loops.
- **FAIL:** stories.js:354 and 371 still call `scrollIntoView({behavior:'smooth'})` unconditionally. This is
  the classic-scroll final quiz (Yusuf). An explicit `behavior` overrides the CSS `scroll-behavior: auto`.

### 9. RTL semantics

**PASS on the mechanics, FAIL on stray strings.**
- The runtime toggle sets `lang=ar` and `dir=rtl` on `<html>` (bilingual.js:44-45), verified on Home,
  a surah and the reader.
- Quranic text carries `lang="ar"`, and Home's Iqra span carries `lang="ar" dir="rtl"`.
- The reader's language segments carry `lang`, and the mini-player's English line carries `lang="en"`.

Mismatches found by a text-node sweep of all 30 pages:
- **The skip link stays "Skip to content" under lang=ar** on all 25 shell pages (index.html:21 and the others;
  no `data-ar`).
- **The app-bar toggle label has no `lang`.** "عربي" sits under lang=en and "English" under lang=ar
  (bilingual.js:89). Its `aria-label` hides this from screen readers, but the visible label is not in the
  accessible name (2.5.3), which matters for voice control. Wrap the label in `<span lang>`, or include it
  in the name.
- **`document.title` stays English in Arabic** on every page; bilingual.js never sets it.
  Screen readers then read the title with the Arabic voice.
- The surah "Learning stage" group label is English-only on 22 pages. The YouTube iframe titles are
  English-only. "آمِين" on Fatiha lacks `lang="ar"` in EN.
- The Arabic greeting puts a Latin name in an RTL heading without `<bdi>` («السلام عليكم يا Sara»,
  home.js:73). This is a nit.

### 12. Name field and PII

**PASS for the v2 fields, FAIL for the legacy modal.**
- Home and reader settings fields have a programmatic `<label>` ("Child's name" / «اسم الطفل»),
  `autocomplete="off"`, `spellcheck="false"` (which keeps Chrome's enhanced spell check from sending text
  to Google) and `maxlength=20`. The name is stored only in `localStorage`.
- I watched all external requests: count.js, YouTube embeds and data URIs. **None carries the name.**
- `document.title` never includes the name, so GoatCounter's title field is clean.
- Web Speech reads only the static voice scripts, which contain no `{name}`.

**FAIL, must fix:** the v1 name modal still opens on the **first visit to any story** when no name is stored.
It is built by `showNameModal()` (stories.js:9-11, 50-96; interactive-story.js:13-15, 85-125) and contradicts
DESIGN 7.6 ("no default name ships... with no name the greeting is just the salam"). Measured:
- On Nuh, Musa and Yusuf the modal **cannot be dismissed without typing a name**. `submitName` ignores empty
  input (stories.js:98-111) and Esc does nothing. The overlay covers the transport, so a child cannot start
  the story without entering personal data.
- On Adam and Ibrahim **two modals stack, with a duplicate `id="nameModal"`**. Both scripts declare
  `showNameModal`, and both call it. An empty submit removes one of them.
- It is a plain `div`: no `role=dialog`, no `aria-modal`, and no label (placeholder only). It has no
  `autocomplete=off` and no `spellcheck=false`. Tab leaves it and reaches the hidden reader controls.

Delete both code paths. The settings sheet already provides the field.

### 13. YouTube iframes

**PASS.** All 27 embeds have a `title` ("Prophet Nuh Story Animation", "Learn Surah Al-Fatiha"...;
for example surah-fatiha.html:253). Should fix: titles are English-only in AR. In the reader, the iframe
sits in an inert slide until its page.

### 14. Zoom 200% and text size, including the Aa control

**PASS with should-fix items.**
- At 640 px (1280 at 200%): no horizontal overflow on any page, in EN or AR.
- At 320 px: no overflow except surah pages, where the Read/Practice/Recite segmented control is 3 px too wide.
  It does not wrap (app.css:744, surah.css:1359).
- At root font size 200%: reader text scales (rem-based `--step-read`) and wraps. `body.reader`'s 112 px
  bottom padding keeps the last line above the transport. The tab bar grows from 65 to 92 px.
- The Aa control keeps the 18 px floor at "small" (`max(1.125rem, ...)`, stories.css v2) and applies to
  Arabic too.

Should fix:
- Surah pages at 200% text, 390: 71 px horizontal overflow (tab-bar items).
- Home in AR at 200% text, 390: the app bar overflows by 31 px. The brand and three actions do not fit;
  let the brand text drop below 400 px.
- Adam and Ibrahim reader bar at 320: the stars counter and effects toggle squeeze the title to **24 px**
  (just "…"). At 200% text the bar wraps to 136 px. Move the stars into the sheet, or hide the name below 360.
