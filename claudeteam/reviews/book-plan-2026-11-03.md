# Printed books on Lulu: pilot and plan (2026-11-03)

Author: book-designer. Status: **pilot built, nothing committed, no artwork generated, no local
model started.** This is a plan for the owner to decide on, not finished books.

## What the owner asked for

Each story and surah on the site as a book you can order from Lulu. The books use the Kids Night
Stories template, interior and cover, and carry a QR code that plays the site's narration.

**How the page maps to the template.**
- Stories:
  - **Cover:** cover.webp; "Prophet *Nuh*…" title; the footer moral as the line.
  - **Pages:** one per scene, with the h2 as the name and the scene paragraphs as the line.
    A `highlight-text` becomes the italic "sound".
  - **End pages:** "What We Learn", then the dua (the Arabic of the dua is set in Amiri).
  - **Left out:** quizzes, taps and drag-and-drop are screen activities and are not printed.
- Surahs:
  - **Cover:** the first verse plate, or an ornament if there is no plate.
  - **First pages:** the first intro card, then the Bismillah.
  - **Verse pages:** one per verse. The English "line" is the translation plus the
    explanation. The English "sound" is the transliteration. The Arabic "sound" is the ayah
    in Amiri.
  - **Last page:** the complete surah.
- Every word is copied from the page. **196 scripture spans across the 27 books were checked
  against their source page: 196 are verbatim, 0 differ.**
- `UI/scripts/verify-scripture.py` was run the same day: **ALL SCRIPTURE MATCHES** (21 surah
  pages plus Ayat al-Kursi, letters tier). The story duas, including Nuh's رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي
  وَلِوَالِدَيَّ, are outside that verifier's scope. They are copied verbatim, but
  `islamic-content-scholar` should confirm them.

### Problems the pilot found

1. **The volume cover's contents list overflows past 4 books.** The template was built for
   volumes of 3-4 short titles. With 5 stories, the last entry (Musa) runs past the frame. On
   Short Surahs II (8 surahs) the last 3 entries are cut off.
   - **None of the ported checks catches this.** check-print-text skips cover spreads, exactly
     as in bedtime-stories.
   - Possible fixes: a small template change (shrink the front picture, or set the contents
     in two columns when there are more than 4 entries), or no more than 4 books per volume.
   - I also propose adding a cover-fit check that measures the same way check-print-text does.
   - **Owner decision #4.**
2. **The site's plates are 16:9. The template's picture box is square.** `object-fit:contain`
   letterboxes them. On an interior page the plate prints 88 x 49.5mm in an 88mm square box,
   with paper above and below, and the cover picture is 96 x 54mm. It reads as a small picture
   on a big page. Options:
   - (a) Accept it.
   - (b) Use a 16:9 picture box for this site's books. This changes the template layout and
     needs owner approval.
   - (c) Render new square print art. This means new compositions, every one reviewed again
     by the scholar, and more render time.
   - I recommend (b).
3. **No Arabic title on a single book's cover.** The template's single-book cover shows the
   English title plus the English and Arabic line, and the Arabic title appears only in a
   volume's contents list. For a bilingual Islamic book the Arabic title should be on the
   cover. Adding it is a template change: owner decision #4.
4. **The title highlight is the template's red** (`#D7263D` on "Nuh"), not a colour from the
   site's palette. Page accents already use the site palette (turquoise-deep, cobalt,
   gold-text, all 4.5:1 or better on the print paper). Only the template's own `--accent`
   default is still red. Owner decision #4.
5. **check-print-text flags 2 false positives on Short Surahs I** (p7 Al-Fatiha 5, p19 Ayat
   al-Kursi part 6). Shadda and other harakat confuse pdftotext's word matching, which the
   bedtime README warns about. I rendered both pages and every word is on the page. They are
   recorded here, not hidden.
6. **Most surah pages have no plate.** 153 of the 235 surah pages carry the gold ornament in
   place of a picture. Al-Fatiha and Al-Ikhlas have no plates at all; their calligraphy panel
   on the site is HTML, not an image. Al-Fatiha, the first book of Short Surahs I, therefore
   also has an ornament on the volume's front cover. A `--front-art` picture for that volume
   would fix this.
7. **Chrome embeds the fonts as Type 3** (Fraunces, Fredoka, Baloo Bhaijaan; Amiri is
   CID TrueType). They are embedded, so check-lulu passes them. The bedtime-stories PDFs are
   made the same way. Some preflight systems warn about Type 3 fonts, so watch for that on
   the first proof upload.
8. Fixed during the pilot: Ayat al-Kursi is one verse. The extractor now labels its cards
   "Part N / الجزء N", as the page does ("piece by piece / جزءاً جزءاً"), not "Verse N".

Why this grouping:
- **Al-Fatiha and Ayat al-Kursi open volume I with the three Quls.** These are the passages
  the Prophet ﷺ recited before sleep (Ayat al-Kursi and the Mu'awwidhat), so volume I is a
  natural bedtime book. Al-Fatiha comes first because every prayer starts with it.
- **The stories stay as one volume.** Splitting them into Prophets I/II gives at best 34 + 24
  pages, and the second half is under 32. A second stories volume only works once there are
  about 3 more prophet stories.
- **Surahs II-IV are grouped by size.** Within each volume they follow mushaf order. Children
  usually learn from An-Nas upwards; reversing the order is an owner choice.
- **Volume I needs a `--front-art` picture.** Its first book, Al-Fatiha, has no plate.
- Grouping several books per volume relies on fixing problem 1 (the contents list). Otherwise
  each volume is capped at 4 books: Short Surahs II would split into two volumes of 4, and the
  stories volume would lose one story, which leaves a 1-story remainder.

## Print-art re-render job (NOT run; needs the owner's go-ahead)

- **Count:** 116 plates (stories: 48 including 5 covers; surahs and Ayat al-Kursi: 68). That
  is every `.webp` under `UI/images/` except `illuminated-frame.webp`. Each is referenced by
  the extracted data.
- **Size needed:** at least 300ppi and at most 600ppi in every box the template uses for a
  16:9 plate:
  - 88-98mm wide on interior pages;
  - 76mm on section dividers;
  - 96mm on the front cover;
  - 66mm in the `long` tier.

  As in bedtime-stories, every master is resampled once to 1400px wide JPEG q90. That gives
  98mm: 363ppi, 88mm: 404ppi, 76mm: 468ppi, 66mm: 539ppi, so every box lands inside the band.
- **Size to render:** Draw Things needs multiples of 64. The only 16:9 sizes that qualify are
  multiples of 1024x576, so render at 2048x1152. That is the same pixel count (2.36MP) as
  bedtime-stories' 1536x1536 run, which did 108 images in about 2 hours.
- **Two ways to get there:**
  - **A. Render at 2048x1152 with the same prompt and seed** (`zlib.crc32("story/key")`).
    Diffusion models do not keep the composition when the resolution changes. The pictures
    will come back different, and every one must go past `islamic-content-scholar` again
    (sacred subjects). **About 116 x ~65s ≈ 2-2.5 hours.**
  - **B. (recommended) Rebuild the approved composition exactly, then upscale.** Re-render at
    the original 1024x576 with the same seed to get back the approved plate, then upscale 2x
    with Draw Things' upscaler (for example `--config-json '{"upscaler":"realesrgan_x2plus_f16.ckpt","upscalerScaleFactor":2}'`;
    confirm the upscaler is downloaded with `draw-things-cli models list --downloaded-only`).
    The composition stays the one the scholar already approved, and a contact-sheet diff
    against the web plate shows any drift. **About 116 x ~50s ≈ 1.5-2 hours.**
- **How it must run** (not how `UI/scripts/build-illustrations.py` runs today, which calls
  `/opt/homebrew/bin/draw-things-cli` directly):
  - through the gated shim `$HOME/.local/bin/draw-things-cli`;
  - `ram-gate status` first, and wait until at least 25% of RAM is free;
  - one image job at a time, nothing else heavy alongside;
  - started by hand only (no schedule);
  - masters go to `print/art-hi/<stories|surahs>/<book>/<plate>.png` (not committed), and
    1400px JPEGs to `print/input/img-hi/…jpg` (committed, like bedtime's `input/img-hi`).
  - `extract-book.py` already refuses to build without them.
- **Optional extra art** (more time, plus scholar review):
  - a volume cover picture for Short Surahs I;
  - back-cover pictures (`--back-art`); the template's back cover is otherwise mostly empty
    paper.

## Cost per volume (PRINTING-GUIDE.md ranges, researched October 2026)

The guide's Lulu figures are for 8.5in square premium colour: paperback about $8-15 and
hardcover about $16-25 per copy, plus about $5-10 shipping. Those figures were for 40-page
volumes. These volumes are 52-68 pages, so plan on the **top of each range** and confirm in
Lulu's own calculator before ordering.

|---|---:|---|---|
| Stories of the Prophets | 60 | ~$12-15+ | ~$22-25+ |
| Short Surahs I | 52 | ~$11-15 | ~$20-25 |
| Short Surahs II | 68 | ~$13-15+ | ~$23-25+ |
| Short Surahs III | 64 | ~$13-15+ | ~$23-25+ |
| Short Surahs IV | 56 | ~$12-15 | ~$21-25 |
| **The set of 5** | 300 | **~$60-75+** | **~$110-125+** |

Shipping is extra. **Order one proof copy of one volume first**: check the colours (prints
come out darker), the Arabic, Amiri's harakat at 22pt, and that the QR codes scan from paper.
This is not a board book; Lulu does not make board books.

## Owner decisions needed

1. **Volume grouping.** Accept the 5 volumes above, or regroup. Decide the order inside the
   surah volumes (mushaf order, or An-Nas upwards) and where Al-Fatiha and Ayat al-Kursi go
   (proposed: volume I).
2. **Paperback or hardcover.** Every volume clears both minimums (32 for perfect bound, 24 for
   hardcover). Hardcover costs about twice as much and survives a child better.
3. **Bilingual (as built) or an Arabic-only right-to-left edition.** An Arabic edition needs a
   right-bound book, mirrored page order and an Arabic-first cover. That is a separate build
   flag, not built here.
4. **Template changes.** The brief says to ask before changing the look:
   - (a) fit more than 4 contents entries on a volume cover (needed for the proposed volumes);
   - (b) a 16:9 picture box instead of letterboxing in a square;
   - (c) the Arabic title on the single-book and divider covers;
   - (d) the title highlight in the site palette (turquoise or cobalt) instead of the
     template red;
   - (e) volume frames: currently the template's Islamic star frame, in pine `#14383A` for the
     stories and cobalt-deep `#1B3C6B` for the surahs.
5. **Print art.** Approve the re-render job (option A or B), and any new cover or back-cover
   pictures. All of it goes past `islamic-content-scholar`.
6. **Frozen printed URLs.** The 30 URLs in `print/input/qr/URLS.txt` will be printed. Once a
   copy is ordered, `UI/story-prophet-*.html`, `UI/surah-*.html`, `UI/ayat-al-kursi.html`,
   `UI/stories.html` and `UI/quran.html` must never be renamed or moved (add a redirect stub if
   they ever are). The codes open the page itself, not a language or slide. A deep link
   (`?lang=ar`) needs `audio-narration-engineer` to confirm the page honours it.
7. **What a surah book contains.** The pilot prints the first intro card, the Bismillah, one
   page per verse (translation + explanation + transliteration + ayah in Amiri) and the
   complete surah. Quizzes, hadith cards, fun facts and tips are left out. Two options:
   - add a "talk about it" page of quiz questions at the end of each book (this would also
     replace blank padding);
   - leave the books as they are.

   **`islamic-content-scholar` must review the surah page plan and the ayah placement**: the
   ayah sits under its Arabic explanation, because the template's "sound" line is the last
   one on the page.
