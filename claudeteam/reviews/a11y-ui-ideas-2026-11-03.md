# Accessibility audit: UI ideas for story slides, hub and surah pages (2026-11-03)

Accessibility Auditor, read-only. This audits the current state and gives a verdict on each item in the
shortlist in `claudeteam/reviews/ui-reference-research-2026-11-03.md`.

**Method.** Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x relative luminance, computed from the tokens in `UI/styles.css:10-56`.
For gradients the worse stop is quoted. Runtime facts come from headless Chrome 156 against
`python3 -m http.server`, at 390px (touch emulation) and 1280px, on `story-prophet-adam.html`,
`surah-fatiha.html`, `quran.html`, `index.html` and `stories.html`.

Findings:
- **Reduced motion is in better shape than the research note suggests.** Every page loads `styles.css`,
  so its global block neutralises all CSS keyframes and transitions in every sheet. The statement that
  interactive-story.css has "no own reduced-motion rules" is true, but those rules are covered anyway.
- **Gap 1: JS smooth scrolling ignores reduced motion.** `scrollIntoView({behavior:'smooth'})` at
  stories.js:354, 371 and surah.js:152, 165. An explicit `behavior` overrides CSS `scroll-behavior`.
- **Gap 2: the `*` selector does not match `::view-transition-*` pseudo-elements.** Any View Transition
  added later (ideas 2 and 10) will animate under reduced motion unless it gets its own rule.
- **WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide), level A, fails for users who have not set the OS preference.**
  `story-prophet-adam.html` runs **30 infinite animations at once** (measured with `document.getAnimations()`),
  with no control to pause them. Bedtime mode (bedtime.js) does not stop them. Other pages: surah 8,
  quran 6, stories 6, index 1.
- The JS-injected background stars and clouds (stories.js:946-975) are animated, and the container
  has no `aria-hidden`. Screen readers announce "star, sparkles, glowing star, dizzy..." at the top of every story.
- `transition: all` appears about 30 times across the sheets (for example stories.css:1090, interactive-story.css:45).
  It also animates `outline`, so the focus ring can lag or "grow" in.

### 1.4 Keyboard and focus

- **Critical: off-screen slides stay in the tab order and the accessibility tree.** 22 focusable controls sit in
  non-active slides, and 0 slides carry `inert` or `aria-hidden`. Measured: tabbing to a button in slide 2
  while slide 1 is shown scrolls the `overflow:hidden` container (`scrollLeft` 0 -> 741). The view then shows
  a slide at `opacity:0` (stories.css:1798-1806) while the counter still says "1". The keyboard user sees a
  blank panel. Code: stories.js:557-567 and 983-994; CSS stories.css:934-960, 1798-1806.
- The focus ring exists but is below 3:1 (see 1.2). Hub cards lift and shadow on `:hover` only
  (styles.css:421-427, 449, 485, 607-610). `:focus-visible` gets only the weak gold ring.
- Not keyboard operable: `.tap-to-reveal` is a `<div>` with a click handler (story-prophet-adam.html:94, 227, 283;
  interactive-story.js:252-256; measured `tabIndex -1`). `.drag-item` is a `<div draggable>` (adam:197-199).
  It has a click alternative (interactive-story.js:359), but it cannot take focus. Slide dots and the narration
  seek bar have the same problem.
- The global arrow-key handler (stories.js:751-764) ignores `e.target`. Arrow keys inside the name input
  (stories.js:68) or any future range input change the slide.
- The "Explore Activities" button on the home page has no handler (index.html:174). It is a dead control.

### 1.5 Screen-reader semantics

- **No slide-change announcement.** There are **zero** `aria-live` or `role=status` regions on any audited page.
  The counter `#currentSlideNum` is updated silently (stories.js:1020-1023). Quiz feedback
  (`.quiz-feedback`, stories.js:193, 214) is not announced either.
- Icon-only controls with no accessible name (the name falls back to the glyph):
  - prev/next `◀` `▶` (stories.js:602-614) is read as "black left-pointing triangle".
  - narration play `▶️`/`⏸️` and auto-play `🔄` (audio-narration.js:34-63) have `title` only. Their title stays
    English in Arabic mode (627).
  - `#soundToggle` `🔊` has `title` only and no `aria-pressed` (story-prophet-*.html, for example adam:60).
  - Correctly labelled: fullscreen (stories.js:784-785), auto-turn (audio-narration.js:613-619), bedtime,
    lang toggle, nav-toggle.
- Decorative emoji that are announced: `.learn-icon` (index.html:147, 152, 157, 162), emoji inside headings
  (index.html:172 "🎨 Fun Activities"), `.tap-icon`/`.fact-icon` (adam:96, 101), `.slide-counter::before` 📖
  (stories.css:1784), the JS background stars and clouds (above). Correctly hidden: floating decorations,
  story icons, scene plates.
- Headings skip from H1 to H3 on `quran.html` (l.72) and `stories.html`.
- Hub `.fade-in` cards are `opacity:0` in CSS (styles.css:746-750) and are revealed only by the inline
  IntersectionObserver (index.html:190-200). If JS fails, or IO never fires, all 11 cards stay invisible.
  This is the known failure mode, still present. surah.js:236-250 shows the correct pattern: hide only when JS
  can bring the cards back.

### 1.6 RTL and language

- `lang` and `dir` update correctly at runtime (bilingual.js:44-45). Verified: after toggling, `lang=ar`, `dir=rtl`.
- **Slide progress text reverts to English in Arabic mode.** Verified: after "next", `.progress-text` reads
  "11% Complete" under `lang=ar` (stories.js:1031-1033). The data-ar string is overwritten, and a screen reader
  reads English with the Arabic voice.
- **Arrow keys and swipes are not mirrored.** In Arabic, ArrowLeft goes to the previous slide
  (verified 2 -> 1), and a left swipe still goes to the next one (stories.js:736-764). The slide track is forced
  LTR (stories.css:1486-1493), so in Arabic the next page still enters from the right.
  `.slide-navigation` is row-reversed (stories.css:1481), but the glyphs are not. The "previous" button
  therefore sits on the right and points left, toward "next".
- Progress dots are forced LTR (stories.css:1502).
- The narration control-bar titles and the "Toggle Sound" title are English only.
- In Arabic mode the body face is Amiri site-wide (styles.css:929). The story pages override it to
  Noto Sans Arabic in an inline `<style>` (adam:15-19). See idea 7.

## Part 2: Verdicts on the shortlist ideas

Legend: SAFE / SAFE WITH CONDITIONS / UNSAFE (for 4-10 year olds)

**1. Calm the existing motion (one-shot entrances, loops of 6s or more, no `transition: all`): SAFE. Do it first.**
This is a fix, not a risk. It is the only practical way to pass WCAG 2.2.2 without adding a pause button.
Conditions:
- No infinite loop inside the reading area. Any loop that remains (for example a slow lantern) is low
  amplitude and stops within 5s, or bedtime mode stops it.
- The rest pose is the final keyframe.
- Replace `transition: all` with an explicit list, and never include `outline`.
- Keep the global reduced-motion block. Delete or `aria-hidden` the JS background stars and clouds (stories.js:946-975).
- Drop fireFlicker's 0.5s brightness flicker (interactive-story.css:158).

**2. Cross-fade slides (250ms, 24px or less, RTL-aware) or `startViewTransition`: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.**
- Non-active slides must be `inert` (or `hidden`). This must ship **with** this change, or before it
  (see defect 1.4). A cross-fade where only the active slide is rendered fixes the blank-panel bug at no extra cost.
- Announce each change in a polite live region: "Page 3 of 18: The Gift of Life" (localised, with
  `lang` matching). Keep focus on the button that was pressed, so a child can tap "next" repeatedly.
- Skip `startViewTransition` when `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')` matches. In CSS, set
  `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce){ ::view-transition-group(*), ::view-transition-old(*), ::view-transition-new(*){ animation:none } }`.
  The global `*` rule does not reach these pseudo-elements.
- The direction follows `dir`: in Arabic, "next" enters from the left. Mirror ArrowLeft/ArrowRight, swipe and
  the arrow glyphs together, or use direction-neutral icons.
- With narration auto-turn on, the transition must not cut off the start of the next clip.

**3. Words appear with narration, karaoke gold word: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS. This is the highest-risk idea.**
- The full sentence is in the DOM from the first paint. Hide words with `opacity`/`color` only, never with
  `display:none`, `visibility:hidden` or by injecting text later. Screen readers and browser "read aloud"
  then get the whole sentence.
- Wrap whole words in inline `<span>`s, never `inline-block`. Never split inside an Arabic word: that breaks
  letter joining and diacritics, and iOS VoiceOver turns each block into a separate swipe stop.
- Show every word immediately when: reduced motion is set; narration is off, muted or failed (the Web Speech
  fallback has no timestamps); the child pauses; the slide is revisited. Add a "show all text" path. A child
  who reads faster than the narrator must not be held back.
- No blur filter on text. The opacity fade lasts 150ms or less.
- The karaoke highlight must not be gold text (2.82:1). Use an ink word on `--gold-wash` (10.90:1), or a
  `--gold-text` underline (5.06:1). Do not change font-weight on the active word, because it reflows the line.
- No live-region announcements per word.

**4. Highlight the verse being recited (surah): SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.**
- The proposed signals are too faint to be the only cue: gold-wash vs card is 1.14:1, and the 1px
  `--rule-strong` is 1.80:1. Add a 3px `border-inline-start` in `--gold-text` (5.06:1) or turquoise (4.05:1),
  plus `aria-current="true"` on the card. `aria-pressed` on `.verse-listen` already exists.
- `scrollIntoView` uses `behavior: reduced ? 'auto' : 'smooth'`. Scroll only when the card is off-screen.
  Never move focus. Pause auto-scroll if the user scrolls.
- Use logical properties so the bar sits on the reading edge in RTL.
- Fix `.surah-listen-all` white on turquoise (4.37:1 at 0.9rem) in the same change, because this feature
  makes it the main control.

**5. Focus cards (dim siblings) and candle-light sheen, hover only: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.**
- Dim only decoration (icon, illustration, shadow), never text. Card body text (slate-500 on card) is
  4.99:1 and has no headroom: even about 10% dimming breaks AA.
- No blur on sibling text.
- Gate it with `@media (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`. Give `:focus-visible` the same lifted state that
  hover has today (styles.css:421-427). If siblings dim, do it with `:has(:focus-visible)` as well.
- The sheen does not follow the pointer under reduced motion. It is decorative (`pointer-events:none`).

**6. Hairlines over shadows, one warm shadow, no pure #fff/#000: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.**
- Hairlines are 1.36-1.80:1. That is fine for decorative card edges. Controls whose shape is only a border
  (secondary CTA, `.verse-listen`, `.stage-btn`) need a text label or fill that identifies them; do not rely
  on the hairline.
- **Fix the focus ring in the same pass.** Replace `outline: 3px solid var(--gold)` with `--gold-text`
  (5.06:1 on parchment, 5.37:1 on card) or a two-tone ring (ink plus parchment). In the header, keep
  parchment (5.88:1). Gold on #0F6B65 is 2.09:1.
- "Remove pure #fff" must not apply to white text on fills. Moving to parchment-coloured text would drop
  white-on-gold from 3.04:1 further. Fix those pairs instead: ink on gold-light, or gold-ink on gold-pale.
- Once shadows are gone, card vs parchment is 1.06:1. Keep the turquoise "Read story" pill as the affordance.

**7. Serif reading face (Literata / Source Serif 4) for narration and translation: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS. Not for the children's story text.**
- Early readers (about 4-7) and dyslexic readers do better with a plain sans with single-storey a/g and a
  clear I/l/1 distinction; the British Dyslexia Association style guide recommends sans serif.
  Keep Nunito (or move to Andika or Atkinson Hyperlegible) for slide text the child reads.
- A serif is acceptable for parent-facing text: parent notes, the explanation of the meaning on surah pages.
- Raise the slide body size from 1rem / 0.95rem mobile (stories.css:991, 1249) to at least 1.125rem.
  Keep line-height at 1.6 or more, weight 400 or more, no light cuts.
- Arabic: Amiri as the site-wide body face (styles.css:929) has a small x-height at text sizes. Use Noto Naskh
  Arabic or Noto Sans Arabic for UI and story text. Keep Amiri / Amiri Quran for scripture. Size Arabic at
  1.1-1.15x the Latin size, and keep line-height at 1.9 or more (1.95 today, styles.css:993-996).
- Keep `display=swap`.

**8. Before/after reveal slider: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.**
- Use a native `<input type="range">` with a visible localised label, an `aria-valuetext` such as
  "Before - 30%", step 10, and Home/End support. The thumb hit area is at least 44x44.
- Also offer two buttons, "Before" and "After", for children who cannot drag.
- The arrow-key handler must ignore events whose target is an input (stories.js:751). The slide swipe
  handlers (stories.js:641-642) must not fire on the slider: use `stopPropagation` and
  `touch-action: none` on the thumb. The container is `touch-action: pan-x` (stories.css:937), which conflicts.
- In RTL, check that the key direction matches the visual direction.
- Both images carry real `alt` text, or a caption describes the change. The scene plates today are
  `alt=""` inside `aria-hidden` (adam:87), which is fine for decoration, but here the picture is the content.

**9. Recap card stack, tap-driven: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.**
- No timer. The top card is a `<button>` or has a "Next card" button. Announce "Card 2 of 5".
- Cards underneath are `inert`.
- Use a static tilt of 3deg or less. Under reduced motion the swap is instant, and the throw direction follows `dir`.
- 44px targets.

**10. Cross-document View Transitions, hub card to story header: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.**
- Put `@view-transition { navigation: auto; }` inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`.
  Add the pseudo-element rule from idea 2.
- 300ms or less. No meaning depends on it. Focus starts at the top of the new page as normal.
- Low a11y risk.

**11. Line-art empty states; shamsa draws itself on surah completion: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.**
- Decorative SVG gets `aria-hidden="true"`.
- Completion is stated in text ("You finished Al-Fatiha!") in a `role=status` region, not only drawn.
- The draw is one-shot and 1.5s or less. It is shown finished under reduced motion.
- Any stroke that carries meaning uses `--gold-text` (5.06:1), not `--gold` (2.82:1).
- The rest pose is the design, since tablets have no hover.

**12. One featured "Tonight's story" card: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.**
- It is static and does not rotate during a visit (no carousel).
- The badge is at least 0.875rem, with passing colours. Today's `.featured::before` is white on gold at
  3.04:1 and 0.68rem.
- Mark "featured" with text, not only colour or a border.

**13. Static lantern glow behind the surah title: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.**
- It is static, so there is no motion risk.
- Measure the white title against the brightest point of the glow. White on gold-light is 1.89:1; the large
  title needs at least 3:1. Keep the glow behind ornament, or cap its lightness under the text.

**14. Gentle illustration parallax: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS, but recommend skipping it.**
- Off under reduced motion, off for coarse pointers and touch, 6px or less, never scroll-linked inside slides.
- It adds vestibular risk for no gain in learning.

Aceternity items mentioned but not shortlisted:
- **Animated EN/AR tab pill: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.** Two buttons with `aria-pressed` (or a radiogroup).
  Each label carries its own `lang` (`<span lang="ar">عربي</span>`). 44px. The pill colours must pass:
  ink on gold is 4.18:1 today and fails.
- **Hand-drawn gold underline for vocabulary: SAFE WITH CONDITIONS.** It must not look like a link unless it is
  one. If it is tappable, make it a `<button>`.
- Items on the research "avoid" list (meteors, vortex, typewriter, magnetic button, infinite marquee and so on):
  agree. Typewriter and glitch text are **UNSAFE**, because text is hidden from screen readers or flickers.
