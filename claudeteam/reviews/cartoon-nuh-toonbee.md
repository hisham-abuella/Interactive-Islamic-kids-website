# Cartoon test: Prophet Nuh on ToonBee (draft, 2026-11-13)

Owner request: a cartoon of one of our stories, made on ToonBee (app.toonbee.ai), using the same
principles as the owner's existing ToonBee project ("Why Dinosaurs Disappeared"): BeeGuide mode,
16:9, 6 scenes (~60 s), 3D Pixar style, a narrator only, and per scene: narration + image prompt +
short video (motion) prompt. That project uses **no characters** and its prompts say "The frame is
unoccupied, with no people", which fits our rule that prophets are never shown.

Narration is taken from the approved story script (UI/scripts/voice-scripts-data.json,
prophet-nuh en slides 0-2, 4-8), shortened; no new claims. The dua slide (9) is left out: no ayah
or dua is voiced. ToonBee's AI voice reads the narration in English; no recitation, **no music**
(owner rule: no music; set music off in step 4).

## Rules applied to every image and video prompt
- No people or human figures anywhere, not even far away or in silhouette; no faces in the sky,
  clouds, waves or rocks; no hands.
- No beams or rays of light coming down from the sky; no glowing figure; nothing that stands for
  Allah or an angel.
- No text, letters or symbols; no cross or X shapes (masts, beams, rigging, window bars); no
  eye-like circles.
- No rainbow, no dove, no olive branch (Bible imagery, not the Quran's account).
- The Ark is a large wooden boat with a closed roof, made of planks and nails.

## Scenes

### 1. The faithful prophet
**Narration:** Long, long ago, Allah chose Prophet Nuh, peace be upon him, to teach his people to be
kind and to worship only Allah, the One who created the stars, the moon and the animals.
**Image:** A wide ancient valley at golden evening light, with a small village of mud-brick houses
with warm lit doorways, palm trees and green hills, under a clear sky where the first stars and a
thin crescent moon appear. Seen from a hillside at a distance. The frame is unoccupied, with no
people, animals or human figures.
**Video:** A slow, gentle push in over the valley as the sky deepens and more stars appear.

### 2. Never giving up
**Narration:** For 950 years, day and night, Prophet Nuh patiently invited his people to believe in
Allah. But sadly, most of them did not want to listen. Still, he never gave up.
**Image:** An empty dirt path winding between the village houses toward an open square, with a
wooden door half open, in soft morning light. The frame is unoccupied, with no people, animals or
human figures.
**Video:** The light shifts from morning to night: shadows move across the path, the sun sets and
the moon rises over the houses, while the camera holds still.

### 3. Building the Ark
**Narration:** Allah told Prophet Nuh to build a huge boat called an Ark, far from any sea. He
trusted Allah completely and worked hard every day, building it exactly as Allah instructed.
**Image:** On a dry plain between hills, a very large wooden boat under construction: a curved hull
of new planks on a timber cradle, stacks of cut logs, a woodworking bench with a saw and a mallet
resting on it. Daylight, dust in the air. The frame is unoccupied, with no people, animals or human
figures.
**Video:** A slow lateral camera move along the length of the hull, revealing how huge the boat is,
with a few wood shavings drifting in the breeze.

### 4. The animals board
**Narration:** When the Ark was ready, a pair of every kind of animal came aboard: two elephants, two
lions, two giraffes, two birds. Two of everything! What a sight!
**Image:** The finished Ark, a large closed wooden boat, with a wide wooden ramp down to the ground.
Pairs of animals walk calmly up the ramp side by side: two elephants, two lions, two giraffes, two
camels, with pairs of small birds flying above. Warm afternoon light. No people or human figures.
**Video:** The animal pairs keep walking slowly up the ramp in a line while the camera rises
gently to show the whole procession.

### 5. The great flood
**Narration:** Then the rain began, more rain than anyone had ever seen. Water came from the sky and
from the ground and covered the land. But the believers and the animals were safe inside the Ark.
**Image:** The closed wooden Ark floating on a wide grey sea of rolling waves under heavy rain and
dark clouds, the tops of distant hills just visible above the water. The Ark is steady and intact.
No people, no figures at windows, no faces in the clouds or waves.
**Video:** The Ark rises and falls gently on the waves as the rain falls, the camera slowly circling
it at a distance.

### 6. A new beginning
**Narration:** After many days, Allah commanded the rain to stop and the water to go down, and the
Ark came to rest safely on Mount Judi. Trust Allah, be patient, and never give up doing good!
**Image:** The wooden Ark resting on the green top of a mountain at sunrise, the water far below
going down to reveal valleys, wet grass shining, the sky clearing to soft blue. Pairs of animals
walk down the slope into the fresh grass. No people or human figures, no rainbow, no dove.
**Video:** The clouds drift apart as the morning light spreads across the mountain, the animals take
a few steps down the slope, and the camera slowly pulls back to a wide view.

## Scholar gate 1 (2026-11-13)

**Verdict: APPROVE WITH CHANGES.** The narration is sound and stays inside the approved script.
Most changes are to the image and video prompts, where some of the wording makes it likely that an
image generator will add forbidden things. Make changes R1-R9 before generating anything. O1 is
optional.

### What was checked and is fine
- **Aqeedah.** Allah chooses, instructs and commands, and nothing more is claimed. Nothing is
  anthropomorphic. "Under Our eyes" (Hud 11:37) is rightly left out, because it is easy to
  misrender for children. "Peace be upon him" follows the first mention of Nuh (scene 1).
- **950 years, day and night.** The number is from al-'Ankabut 29:14 ("a thousand years less fifty").
  "Night and day" is from Nuh 71:5. Both match the approved script, slide 2.
- **Water from the sky and from the ground.** This is al-Qamar 54:11-12 (gates of the sky opened,
  the earth burst with springs). "Covered the land" is more careful than the approved script's
  "the whole earth" and should stay.
- **Pairs.** "Of every kind, two" is Hud 11:40 and al-Mu'minun 23:27. Naming elephants, lions and
  giraffes is only an illustration, which is acceptable.
- **The Ark.** Planks and nails (al-Qamar 54:13) is correct.
- **Judi and the rain told to stop.** This is a fair paraphrase of Hud 11:44. "After many days" claims
  no number, which is correct.
- **The omissions are right for ages 5-11.** No drowning, no son of Nuh, no wife.
- **Bible-only imagery.** The narration has no Bible-only details. The no-rainbow, no-dove and
  no-olive-branch rule is correct. The mountain is Judi, not Ararat, so it must not be named or
  captioned as anything else.
- **No people at all.** This is the right call; keep it strict. A distant figure or silhouette
  in a Nuh cartoon will be read as Nuh or as a believer with him, and in the flood scene any figure
  would suggest drowning. The site does not depict prophets or the believers with them at any
  distance. "Unoccupied" is the correct standard.
- **English AI narrator.** This is acceptable. The narration contains no ayah, no translation
  presented as an ayah, and no dua (slide 9 is rightly dropped). The scene 6 line "Allah commanded
  the rain to stop..." is a narrated paraphrase, not a recitation, so the no-synthetic-recitation
  rule is kept.
- **Age framing.** The flood narration ends on "safe inside the Ark", which is good. The flood image
  needs softening; see R7.

### Main prompt risk: do not name forbidden things in a positive prompt
Image models often ignore "no". Writing "no rainbow, no dove" or "no faces in the clouds" puts
rainbows, doves and faces into the prompt. Keep the global rules list above as the sight-check
checklist, but do not paste it into the generator. In the image prompts, describe what *is* there
(mastless, clear even sky, solid planks), and keep only the owner's tested sentence "The frame is
unoccupied, with no people or human figures." If ToonBee has a negative-prompt field, put this
there:
`people, person, human, figure, silhouette, face, hands, text, letters, writing, cross, crucifix, rainbow, dove, white bird, olive branch, sun rays, light rays, sunbeams, god rays, glowing light, halo, lightning, mast, sail, rigging, window bars`

Three more risks, with the fixes below:
- **Masts.** Every boat prompt needs an explicit "mastless, no sails". Otherwise the model adds a mast
  with a yardarm, which is a cross.
- **Light and atmosphere.** "Dust in the air", "sunrise" and "clouds drift apart as light spreads"
  are the standard triggers for light beams from the sky, and after rain they also trigger
  rainbows.
- **Openings and frames.** Windows, plank doors, scaffolding and exposed hull ribs produce cross
  shapes and window bars. Windows on the Ark also invite figures or faces looking out.
